Plastic waste includes bottles, containers, packaging materials and other plastic products. Common recyclable plastics include PET, HDPE and PP. Plastic should be separated from organic and hazardous waste before processing. Clean and dry plastic is generally easier to recycle. Contaminated plastic may require washing or pre-treatment. Suitable plastic can be sent to a plastic recycling facility. Non-recyclable plastic may be considered for waste-to-energy treatment where permitted.
